## About

I am a CIFRE PhD student in Machine Learning at Murex AI Research, in collaboration with Université Paris Dauphine – PSL.

My research focuses on unsupervised and representation learning for complex and heterogeneous data.

In particular, I study:
- irregular multivariate time series  
- latent structure recovery from partially observed systems  
- alignment of heterogeneous data sources  

---

## Background

I am a normalien in mathematics from ENS Paris-Saclay, and I hold master’s degrees in:
- Machine Learning (MVA)  
- Statistical and Financial Engineering (ISF)

## Research Interests

More broadly, I am interested in:
- representation learning  
- generative models  
- time series clustering  
- learning with limited supervision
