I am a CIFRE PhD student in Machine Learning at **Murex AI Research**, in collaboration with **Université Paris Dauphine – PSL**.

My work focuses on **unsupervised learning** and **probabilistic modeling** for complex and heterogeneous data. In particular, I study how to model and align irregular multivariate time series, and how to recover latent structures from partially observed systems.

I am a **normalien in mathematics** from **ENS Paris-Saclay**, and I hold master’s degrees in **Machine Learning (MVA)** and **Statistical and Financial Engineering (ISF)**.

More broadly, I am interested in representation learning, generative models, time series, and learning with limited supervision.
