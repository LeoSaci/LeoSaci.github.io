About

I am a PhD student in Machine Learning at Murex AI Research, in collaboration with Université Paris Dauphine – PSL. My work focuses on unsupervised learning and probabilistic modeling for complex and heterogeneous data.

In particular, I study how to model and align irregular multivariate time series, and how to recover latent structures from partially observed systems. More broadly, I am interested in representation learning, generative models, and learning with limited supervision.

Research

My PhD focuses on unsupervised learning for complex temporal systems, with an emphasis on heterogeneous and irregular multivariate time series. I develop probabilistic and generative approaches to recover latent dynamics from partially observed systems and to leverage weak supervision signals for clustering and alignment.
